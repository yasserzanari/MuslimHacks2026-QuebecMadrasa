# Madrasa Québec — Design context

## Product register

Authenticated parent dashboard for Muslim homeschooling families in Québec.

## Visual direction

- Calm, trustworthy, family-oriented interface.
- Deep forest green for navigation and primary actions.
- Mint and cream surfaces for learning content and soft hierarchy.
- Amber only for attention states and the next recommended action.
- Warm serif headings paired with readable system sans-serif body text.
- Rounded cards, generous spacing, and short practical copy.

## Dashboard signature

The parent sees one clear next action first, then a compact family pulse: tasks, children progress, and the nearest Québec deadline.

## Interaction principles

- Every important card leads to an existing route or has a clear unavailable state.
- Parent actions are explicit; AI-generated content always requires review.
- The dashboard works in French and English, with French as the Québec default.
- Child data stays scoped to the selected family and remains non-diagnostic.
